## 🛍️ Task 2: Digital Transformation of the Retail Guest Experience

* **Concept Name:** **FitFinder & Endless Aisle Interactive Kiosk**
* **Core Problem Solved:** Reduces store walkouts and lost revenue caused by out-of-stock sizes/colors while minimizing fitting room congestion.
* **System Architecture & Features:**
  * **Endless Aisle Integration:** Real-time bi-directional inventory synchronization connecting local retail stock with regional warehouse distribution hubs.
  * **AR Fit Assistant:** 3D digital garment mapping and body silhouette measurement tools built directly into an in-store mirror interface.
  * **Seamless Mobile POS:** Contactless one-touch digital checkout offering instant in-store reserve or direct-to-door expedited delivery.
* **Deliverable:** [`Task-2-Retail-Guest-Experience/Task2_Digital_Product_Concept_Submission.pdf`](./Task-2-Retail-Guest-Experience/Task2_Digital_Product_Concept_Submission.pdf)

## 📊 Task 4: Ambassador Marketing Performance Analysis (Q4 Review)

* **Objective:** Clean, calculate, and evaluate campaign return on investment (ROI), customer acquisition cost (CAC), and lead conversion rates across 8 acquisition channels to guide strategic budget reallocations for upcoming quarters[cite: 5, 7].
* **Core Metrics Modeled:**
  * Conversion Rate = Conversions ÷ Leads[cite: 7]
  * Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) = Spend ÷ Conversions[cite: 7]
  * Return on Investment (ROI) = (Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend[cite: 7]

### 💡 Strategic Takeaways & Budget Recommendations
1. **Scale TikTok Creator Recruitment:** Reallocate 35–40% of the Q1 marketing budget to short-form video creators to capitalize on industry-leading viral reach and lowest acquisition costs ($8.82 CAC, +233.33% ROI)[cite: 7].
2. **Expand Email Nurture Funnels:** Deploy creator-curated email workflows to leverage high-margin retention (+170.00% ROI) at low fixed operational spend ($2,000)[cite: 7].
3. **Restructure In-Person Activations:** Preserve the top-performing conversion rate (33.04%) while lowering production overhead ($19.74 CAC) through co-branded ambassador spaces and digital QR check-in attribution[cite: 7].
